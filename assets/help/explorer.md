# Explorer

The Explorer is a knowledge web over the app's bundled study datasets: every **person**, **place**, **event**, **topic**, and **passage** gets its own page, and the pages all link to each other. Start anywhere and follow the connections—from a person to the events in their life, from an event to where it happened, from a place to everyone mentioned there, from a chapter to its topics. It works completely offline.

Open it from the hamburger menu (App Drawer) → **Explorer**.

## Searching
Type at least two letters into the search box to search everything at once—people (including alternate names), places, events, topics, and passages. You can also type a reference like `John 1` to open that chapter's page directly. Tap any result to open its page.

## Entity Pages
Each page is a set of cards showing everything the datasets know, and every entry on a card is a link to another page:

- **People** — an Easton's biography, family members (tap a name to open their page), the events they took part in, a map of the places in their story, and every verse where they appear.
- **Places** — an interactive map, the events that happened there, the people mentioned there, dictionary definitions, and the verses that mention it.
- **Events** — when it happened, the scripture account, who was there, and a map of where it took place.
- **Topics** — the topic's entries from Nave's Topical Bible with their passages, plus dictionary definitions.
- **Passages** — the people, places (with a map), events, and topics found in a chapter, alongside every installed commentary's entries for it and **your own notes** anchored there.

Tap any verse chip to jump straight to that passage in the Reader.

## Breadcrumb Trail
As you drill from page to page, a breadcrumb bar tracks your path. Tap any crumb to jump back to that point, use the back arrow to step back once, or tap the home icon (or the search icon in the app bar) to start a new search. The trail is remembered while the app is open, so reopening the Explorer resumes right where you left off.

> 💡 **Tip:** For focused, chapter-tied views of the same data while you read, see the **Topics**, **Places**, and **People** tools in the Study Tools panel. The Explorer is the free-roaming version—best when one question leads to another.
