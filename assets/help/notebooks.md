# Notebooks

Notebooks are folders of rich-text pages—for anything longer than a single Scratch pad entry or sermon, like a multi-week Bible-study series, a topical study with one page per sub-topic, or a set of class notes you want kept together.

## Creating a Notebook
1. Open the Notebooks tool from the Study Tools menu.
2. Tap **+** to create a notebook, and give it a title, cover color, and cover icon.
3. Open it, then tap **+** again to add pages. Each page gets its own title and the same rich-text editor used by Sermons and Journals.

## Organizing Pages
- **Reorder:** Drag a page by its handle to move it up or down within the notebook.
- **Insert Scripture:** From a page's editor, tap **Insert Scripture** to look up a passage and drop its reference and text in at the cursor.
- **Dictate:** A microphone button beside the toolbar lets you dictate instead of type. (Not available on Windows or Linux desktop.)
- **Drag verses in:** Select verses in the Reader and drag them straight into a page to insert them, no copy/paste needed.

## Organizing & Finding Notebooks
- **Pin to top:** Keep a notebook you're actively using at the top of the list regardless of sort order.
- **Sort:** Order the list by title (A–Z or Z–A) or by when it was last updated (newest or oldest first).
- **Tags:** Tap **Manage Tags** to file a notebook under tags like `#romans` or `#membership-class`—the same tags used across the whole app.
- **Search:** Type in the search box to filter by title, or type `#tag` to filter by tag.

## Revision History
Every page keeps a history of past versions. Open **Revision History** to look back at—or restore—an earlier draft.

## Exporting & Printing
Export a single page or an entire notebook as:
- **PDF** — a clean plain-text layout.
- **HTML** — retains all of your rich formatting.
- **Markdown** — headings, lists, and bold/italic preserved.
- **Plain Text** — raw, unformatted text.

Use **Save to Folder** to write the file to disk (on Android this opens the system folder picker; on iOS it opens the Share sheet instead), **Print** to send it to a connected printer, or **Share** to hand it to another app.

## Syncing & Conflicts
Notebooks and their pages sync across your devices the same way Sermons and Journals do. If a page changes on another device while you have it open here, a banner lets you choose **Use their version** or **Keep mine**—whichever you don't pick is saved to Revision History either way, so nothing is lost.
