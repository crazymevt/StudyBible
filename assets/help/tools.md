# Study Tools

The side panel (or the bottom sheet on mobile) contains a powerful suite of tools to help you study the Scriptures. The navigation rail groups them into four sections so they're easy to scan: **Study** (reference works), **My Work** (your notes, highlights, scratch pad, and sermons), **Plans** (reading plans and devotionals), and **Explore** (topics, places, people, harmony, and media).

## Compare
Select a single verse in the Reader to view how it is translated across *every single Bible* you have downloaded, stacked vertically.

## Cross-References
View a list of related verses for the chapter you are currently reading. Tapping a cross-reference will open it in a preview window, allowing you to read it in context without losing your place in the main Reader.

## Commentaries
Read verse-by-verse commentary from classic theologians like Matthew Henry or John Wesley. You must first download commentary modules from the Content Manager.

## Dictionaries
Search for specific words or topics across all your downloaded dictionaries. This is great for doing word studies or looking up cultural context.

## Reading Plans
Track your progress through reading plans, with a checklist for each day and a progress bar. Tap **Create Another Plan** (or **Create New Plan** when you have none) to generate a custom plan—choose the books or whole-Bible scope and how long you want it to take, and the app builds the daily schedule for you. You can run several plans at once and delete any you've finished with.

## Devotionals
Read daily devotionals (like Spurgeon's *Morning and Evening*). Use the day arrows to move through the readings, or jump back to **Today** at any time. Install devotional modules from the Content Manager.

## Topics
Explore the Biblical topics referenced in the chapter you are reading, with the related passages for each.

## Places
See the geographical locations mentioned in the current passage on an interactive map, and jump between the verses that mention them.

## People
Meet the people in the passage you're reading—over 3,000 individuals from the Theographic Bible Metadata knowledge graph, bundled offline. The panel shows who is mentioned in the current chapter, and a search box finds anyone by name (alternate spellings included). Open a person for their profile: lifespan, family members you can tap through to, a biography from Easton's, the events of their life, and every verse group where they appear.

### Timeline
From the People panel, tap **Timeline** for a full-screen, interactive chart of Bible history: a lifespan bar for every dated person over a shared time axis you can pan and zoom, with dated events marked along it. Tap a bar to open that person, or an event marker to read its account. The **Events** toggle switches to a searchable, chronological list of events—handy when the chart gets dense—with jump-to-passage and jump-to-timeline buttons on each one.

## Gospel Harmony
Browse the life of Jesus as a chronological list of events, each showing which of the four Gospels record it. Open an event to read all its parallel accounts side by side, and tap the book icon on any account to jump to that passage in the Reader. While reading one of the Gospels, select a verse and tap **Parallels** in the action bar to see the other Gospels' accounts of the same event. The event arrangement is adapted from classic public-domain harmonies (A. T. Robertson, 1922).

## Read Aloud
Have the current chapter read to you using your device's built-in text-to-speech voices, with adjustable reading speed. Because it relies on the voices already on your device, **Read Aloud works completely offline**—ideal for listening on a plane or away from signal.

## Audio
Listen to professionally narrated, chapter-by-chapter audio Bibles, with multiple voices, adjustable playback speed, and a sleep timer. This is different from **Read Aloud**: Audio is real human narration streamed from the web, while Read Aloud is your device's synthetic voice working offline.

> 🌐 **Needs internet** — Audio is streamed on demand, so playback requires a connection. (Read Aloud does not.)

## Media
Browse and play related video media (such as teaching clips and dramatizations) tied to the chapter you are reading.

> 🌐 **Needs internet** — Media is streamed from the web, so an internet connection is required.

## Scratch
A quick, no-friction pad for rough notes and half-formed ideas. It uses the same rich-text editor as Sermons and Journals, saves as you type, and turns any Bible reference you type (like `John 3:16`) into a tappable link.

The Scratch pad is **device-local and never syncs**—it's your private workspace on this device. When an idea is ready to grow, tap **Promote to Sermon** to start a full sermon from its contents (the pad is left untouched), or **Clear** to wipe the pad and start fresh.

> 💡 **Tip:** The Topics, Places, and People tools stay tied to the chapter you're reading. To roam the same datasets freely—following links from a person to their events to the places involved—open the **Explorer** from the App Drawer. See the **Explorer** help topic.
