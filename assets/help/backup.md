# Backup & Restore

Because StudyBible is an offline-first application, your personal data (journals, sermons, notes, notebooks, and media attachments) lives entirely on your device. We provide robust backup and sync tools to ensure you never lose your data.

## Creating a Backup
Open the **Backup & Restore** screen from the hamburger menu (App Drawer) and tap **Create Backup** to save your highlights, notes, bookmarks, journals, prayers, sermons, notebooks, media attachments, and reading progress to a single file.

- Turn on **Include downloaded content** to bundle your installed Bible translations, commentaries, and dictionaries into the same file too—handy for moving to a new device without redownloading everything, though it can make the file much larger.
- On Android you'll pick a destination folder (Storage Access Framework); on iOS the file goes through the Share sheet (e.g. **Save to Files**); on desktop you get a normal save dialog.

## Restoring a Backup
Tap **Select Backup File** and choose a previously created backup. You'll see what's in it—when it was made, its size, and whether user data and downloaded content are included—before you confirm.

> ⚠️ **Restoring replaces all current data on the device.** It is not a merge. If you use sync, run a sync first so any recent changes are captured before you restore.

After a restore completes, close and reopen the app for the change to take effect.

## Google Drive Sync
The easiest way to sync across devices: open **Settings → Sync** and tap **Connect Google Drive**. Your notes, highlights, sermons, notebooks, media attachments, and reading progress sync through a private app folder in your Google Drive, shared by every device you connect with the same account. Tap **Disconnect** at any time to stop.

## Sync Folder
Prefer to keep sync out of Google? Configure a **Sync Folder** in the Settings instead—any folder your devices share works.
- If you set your Sync Folder to an iCloud Drive directory, StudyBible will automatically sync your changes seamlessly in the background!

## Auto Sync
By default, sync runs when you press the **Sync** button. Turn on **Settings → Sync → Auto sync** to have the app sync by itself: once shortly after it starts, and then on a schedule you choose (every 15 minutes by default) for as long as it stays open. This keeps the Dashboard's **Continue reading** card and your notes, highlights, and progress fresh across devices without thinking about it. Auto sync is silent — if a sync fails (say, you're offline), it simply tries again at the next interval.

> 🌐 **Needs internet** — Writing the backup file itself is offline, but your cloud drive (iCloud, Google Drive, etc.) needs a connection to upload those changes and pull them down on your other devices.
